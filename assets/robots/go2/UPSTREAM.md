# Unitree Go2 model provenance

The model and mesh assets in this package were copied from the official
[`unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco`](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco/tree/main/unitree_robots/go2)
repository at revision `ae6a8403e272733e9996ef59990880330496177f`.

`go2.xml` and the OBJ files under `assets/` are unmodified upstream files.
They are distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license in `LICENSE.unitree`.
`controller.js`, `robot.json`, and `index.json` are project-local integration
files and are not part of the upstream Unitree model.
